# Scientific and cultural assumptions register

This register makes model assumptions reviewable. It is not a bibliography or approval record. Every item remains a teaching hypothesis until its source, geographic applicability, and local relevance are reviewed.

| Assumption | Current use | Required evidence or review | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meaning and preferred use of Pte Oyate and cultural framing | Project narrative | Appropriate OLC/OST cultural and governance authority | Draft; do not present as approved |
| NLCD class-to-suitability scores | Vegetation component | Habitat evidence, classification accuracy, field observations, local review | Teaching defaults |
| NDVI trend represents improving or declining forage | Vegetation adjustment | Seasonal forage measurements, sensor/scale analysis, field validation | Unvalidated proxy |
| Soil capability crosswalk represents grazing capacity | Soils component | Spatial gSSURGO attributes, units, range specialist review, local validation | Incomplete; uniform fallback prohibited in BHSI |
| Slope classes represent habitat use | Topography component | Applicable movement literature, field observations, local expertise | Teaching defaults |
| Daily water use and 5 km maximum distance | Water component | Population- and season-specific evidence; reliability, access, and infrastructure data | Teaching default |
| NHD features represent usable water | Water component | Seasonality, flow, quality, access, and field validation | Known limitation; not established |
| 95°F and 105°F represent stress thresholds | Climate component | Applicable veterinary evidence, humidity and exposure context, local review | Teaching defaults |
| Selected climate model/scenario represents local future conditions | Climate component | Multi-model ensemble, scenario set, baseline, bias and uncertainty analysis | Incomplete |
| Component weights represent ecological importance | Composite BHSI | Scientific synthesis, sensitivity analysis, approved local objectives | Hypothetical only |
| Top 30% represents high suitability | Candidate-region selection | Distributional rationale, sensitivity, field validation, local review | Hypothetical only |
| 500 acres is a meaningful minimum region | Connected-region filter | Herd goals, management feasibility, ecology, tenure, fencing, water, and authority | Hypothetical only |

Before an external presentation, replace “teaching default” with a citation and review record or remove the claim. Record reviewer, date, scope, and permitted use separately; do not infer approval from a completed technical test.

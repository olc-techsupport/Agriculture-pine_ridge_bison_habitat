# Draft data governance statement

This repository analyzes public environmental data describing the Pine Ridge Reservation and ecologically relevant context. Public availability does not remove the responsibility to represent Oglala Lakota lands, waters, relatives, and communities accurately and respectfully.

This wording, including terminology for Pte Oyate and the applicable governance frameworks, is a working draft. It must not be represented as approved by OST, OLC, a bison program, or a Research Review Board. The responsible review authority and contact remain to be confirmed.

## Current data boundary

Only public source data and locally generated screening products belong in the public repository. No OST-controlled operational data, tenure information, culturally sensitive knowledge, or field observations are included or authorized for inclusion.

Before such information is used, a separately approved process must identify the responsible steward, intended collective benefit, storage and access roles, allowed analyses, prohibited uses, review and publication authority, retention requirements, and whether derived products may leave approved infrastructure.

## Public data and territorial interpretation

The Census AIANNH geometry is a reproducible statistical boundary, not a legal determination of jurisdiction or a substitute for OST-defined lands and place descriptions. Federal land-cover, soil, elevation, hydrography, and climate products reflect their producers' classifications, scales, omissions, and purposes. They do not replace field observation or locally held ecological knowledge.

Mapped water is not necessarily reliable or accessible water. Satellite vegetation class is not a direct measure of forage condition. A connected high-scoring raster region is not an approved restoration priority, viable management unit, or statement about where bison belong.

## Review and release

Until the appropriate OLC/OST review process is confirmed and completed:

- outputs are educational and screening-level;
- weights, thresholds, scores, and minimum areas are unapproved assumptions;
- precise candidate-region outputs must not be externally distributed;
- findings must not be attributed to OST or OLC as approved conclusions; and
- cultural, biological, management, and policy interpretations require appropriate evidence and review.

## Frameworks under consideration

CARE, FAIR, IEEE 2890-2025, Local Contexts, and OCAP® may be useful reference points. Naming them does not mean OST or OLC has adopted them. OCAP® was developed in a Canadian First Nations context; its local applicability must be determined by the appropriate OLC/OST authority.

- [ ] Confirm preferred names for OST, Oglala Lakota people, OLC programs, Pine Ridge, and Pte Oyate.

## Scientific and management review

- [ ] Review every biological, cultural, hydrologic, climate, and management assumption and citation.
- [ ] Confirm locally appropriate land-cover scores, distance-to-water assumptions, slope scores, heat thresholds, component weights, suitability percentile, and minimum patch area.
- [ ] Require spatially resolved soil, vegetation, and climate evidence before producing candidate-patch rankings.
- [ ] Validate mapped water reliability, access, tenure, fencing, roads, and field conditions.
- [ ] Confirm that eight-neighbor raster connected components are documented consistently.

## Instructional review

- [ ] Pilot every notebook with a learner new to Python or GIS.
- [ ] Check timing, prerequisites, role rotation, checkpoints, and contribution activities.
- [ ] Confirm that prompts separate observation, interpretation, evidence, and authority.
- [ ] Record pilot feedback and revisions.

## Technical and release checks

- [ ] Run `python -m pytest` in a clean environment.
- [ ] Confirm notebooks are committed without saved outputs.
- [ ] Confirm the provenance manifest identifies every deliberate release artifact.
- [ ] Confirm all BHSI components are spatially varying and provenance identifies source type and fallback status.

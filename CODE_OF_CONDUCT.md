# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive learning and collaboration environment. Treat documentation, stewardship, field knowledge, review, and questioning as technical contributions. Do not use review as a test of belonging, and do not pressure participants to share restricted information or community knowledge.

Project-specific concerns should be raised with the designated OLC project contact once that role is confirmed. Governance, cultural, or release questions should pause the work until the appropriate OLC/OST authority provides direction.
